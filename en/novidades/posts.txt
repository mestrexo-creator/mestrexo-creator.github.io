=== Nov 27, 2026 | A new game in the forge: Golden Key
While TAMBOKEN is in closed testing, the workshop has already started the next game. The name is still a working title: **Golden Key**.

It's a 3D action and exploration game with the same pixel-painted look as TAMBOKEN. Instead of levels, there's a world to explore: a village at the center, roads, graveyards, mines and dark dungeons, with loot, a blacksmith to craft and upgrade gear and, at the bottom of it all, a dragon.

You pick one of three heroes:
- **Ryzen**, the sword-and-shield warrior;
- **Mestre**, the old staff-wielding wizard;
- **Raya**, the archer and assassin.

What's done so far:
- Ryzen's training room, with a sword combo, shield, dodge, timed parry and a spinning special.
- The first monsters of the Crypt: sword skeleton, skeleton archer, a grabbing zombie, the helmeted Captain, the Lich with his staff and the Skeleton King, a three-phase boss. An ogre is ready too.
