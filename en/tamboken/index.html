The forge was empty

Bento Hayashi came back from training in the hills to find his father's forge empty. Colonel Vapor's Ferro-Sol company took Kenzo Hayashi before dawn and left tin-can henchmen running the town of Vila Poeira.

With his father's katana and his mother's revolver, Bento crosses the West, the frozen North, the bandit South and the Japanese port of Kaminari to find out what the Colonel wants to build with a blacksmith's hands.

And his father has kept a secret for twenty years.

Real action

Deflect bullets

Swing your sword at the right moment and send the shot back to whoever fired it.

Sword combos

Spin slashes, dashes, sky drops and the Sunset: a lit cigar that makes everything faster.

Six bullets

Short on ammo, zero patience. Every shot counts.

Movie-style bosses

Baron Caldeira, Kenji Trilho, Iara the Cattle Queen, the Scarecrow King… and they all get enraged at the end of the fight.

A Brazilian Wild West

A saloon with dancers and bar brawls (no guns: house rules!), high-noon duels on your father's pocket watch, horse rides, trains, cattle stampedes, ninjas at night and haunted cornfields. Over 160 missions.

For everyone

  • 4 difficulty levels, from Apprentice to Legend, plus New Game+.
  • Achievements, Daily Challenge and hidden Sheriff Stars.
  • "Painted pixel" look inspired by the PS1.
  • Text in 11 languages, including English.
  • No ads. Plays offline.
  • Free to start. The Full Game is a one-time purchase.
